\chapter{Glossary and Notation}
\label{ch:glossary}

\section*{Terms}
\begin{description}[nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape, leftmargin=3.2cm, style=nextline]
  \item[activity clock] the quantities that describe how recently and how often a market's
  mid has changed: the time since the last mid change, the number of mid changes and of
  book events in the last minutes and hour.  The strongest predictors of whether the mid
  will move (\cref{sec:expl:activity}).
  \item[balanced accuracy] the mean of the per-class recalls of a classifier; $1/3$ for a
  constant three-class rule (\cref{sec:models:protocol}).
  \item[block] the unit of time of the Polygon blockchain, about two seconds; the time step
  of the stateful model (\cref{sec:data:blocks}).
  \item[calibration (offsets)] two additive constants on the \textsc{down} and \textsc{up}
  logits of a model, chosen on the even validation hours and applied to the odd ones and
  vice versa, so that every model's decision rule is placed the same way before models are
  compared (\cref{sec:models:protocol}).
  \item[central / extreme samples] samples whose mid is inside $[0.05, 0.95]$ and outside
  it (\cref{sec:expl:level}).
  \item[CLOB] central limit order book, the matching mechanism of Polymarket: off-chain
  order matching, on-chain settlement (\cref{sec:pm:mechanics}).
  \item[conditional token] an outcome token of a market; a pair of complementary tokens is
  minted from one unit of collateral and the winning one is redeemed for it after resolution
  (\cref{sec:pm:mechanics}).
  \item[control (no-state)] the LightGBM model on the sixty-one hand-made features, the
  stateless reference of every comparison (\cref{sec:stateless:control}).
  \item[cross-fitting] fitting a quantity (offsets, tree log-probabilities, a combination
  weight) on one part of the data and applying it to another, so that no sample is
  evaluated with a quantity fitted on itself.
  \item[deduplicated samples] at most one sample per market per fifteen minutes; the subset
  on which samples are closest to independent (\cref{sec:data:labels}).
  \item[direct path] a zero-initialised linear layer from the lookback price features (and
  the hand features) to the three logits, which contains Mean Reversion as a special case
  (\cref{sec:models:skip}).
  \item[fill] a matched trade, recorded on-chain with maker, taker, token, side, quantity
  and price; the rows of the transactions table.
  \item[hierarchical memory] the minute and hour states of a market, each updated by its
  own GRU cell at its own period (\cref{sec:models:memory}).
  \item[rank-gauss transform] the monotone map of a feature to its rank and then through the
    inverse normal distribution, fitted on training rows; it removes heavy tails and scale
    before distance-based methods (\cref{ch:structure}).
  \item[hour-block bootstrap] resampling of the 24 validation hours with replacement to
  obtain confidence intervals for differences between models (\cref{sec:models:protocol}).
  \item[lead--lag] whether the changes of one contract precede those of another;
  measured at the minute scale between sibling contracts (\cref{sec:expl:siblings}).
  \item[lookback] the change of the mid over a fixed past span (15 min, 1, 3, 6, 12 h).
  \item[Mean Reversion-$H$] the rule that predicts the opposite of the sign of the change
  over the past $H$, and \textsc{const} if the price did not move (\cref{sec:models:baselines}).
  \item[mid price] the average of the best bid and the best ask of the Yes-side book.
  \item[residual model] the stateful model that receives the cross-fitted tree
  log-probabilities as inputs and adds them to its logits (\cref{sec:models:residual}).
  \item[resolution] the decision of a market's outcome through UMA's optimistic oracle
  after the event (\cref{sec:pm:life}).
  \item[sibling contracts] markets of the same event: the two sides of a game, the
  candidates of an election, the strikes of a ladder (\cref{sec:prep:features}).
  \item[stateful / stateless model] a model that keeps a state per market updated from the
  stream, against one that reads a table of features at the sample time
  (\cref{ch:stateless,ch:stateful}).
  \item[TabM] a parameter-efficient ensemble of multilayer perceptrons sharing one
  backbone, the tabular network used in \cref{sec:stateless:tabular}.
  \item[tensor row] one 52-feature description of a market's book at one book event, the
  unit of the derived order-book tensors (\cref{sec:data:tensors}).
  \item[time-first encoder] the window encoder that reads each book quantity as its own
  series before mixing quantities (\cref{sec:models:encoders}).
  \item[token orientation] whether the book (and the label) refers to token 1 or token 2
  of the market; the sign of a trade is corrected accordingly (\cref{sec:data:quality}).
\end{description}

\section*{Acronyms}
\begin{description}[nosep, font=\normalfont\bfseries, leftmargin=2.2cm, style=sameline]
  \item[AMI] adjusted mutual information between two partitions of the markets.
  \item[AUC] area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
  \item[BA] balanced accuracy.
  \item[CI] confidence interval (95\%, hour-block bootstrap unless stated).
  \item[CLOB] central limit order book.
  \item[CT-DGNN] continuous-time dynamic graph neural network, the name of the stateful model in the code.
  \item[GDELT] Global Database of Events, Language and Tone, the news source of \cref{sec:external:news}.
  \item[GRU] gated recurrent unit.
  \item[kNN] $k$-nearest-neighbour classifier.
  \item[LDA] linear discriminant analysis.
  \item[LOB] limit order book.
  \item[MI] mutual information.
  \item[MLP] multilayer perceptron.
  \item[MR] Mean Reversion-$H$.
  \item[OFI] order-flow imbalance.
  \item[PCA] principal component analysis.
  \item[PLE] piecewise-linear encoding of numeric features.
  \item[TBPTT] truncated backpropagation through time.
  \item[TSFM] time-series foundation model.
  \item[t-SNE] t-distributed stochastic neighbour embedding.
  \item[UMA] the optimistic oracle protocol that resolves Polymarket markets.
  \item[UMAP] uniform manifold approximation and projection.
  \item[USDC] the dollar stablecoin used as collateral.
\end{description}

\section*{Notation}
\begin{description}[nosep, font=\normalfont, leftmargin=3.2cm, style=sameline]
  \item[$\mathcal{M}$, $\mathcal{W}$] the sets of markets and wallets; $m$, $w$ one of each (\cref{sec:bg:notation}).
  \item[$(t_i, \mathbf{b}_i)$] the book stream of a market: the time of its $i$-th event and the 52 numbers that describe the book just after it.
  \item[$H$] the prediction horizon (60 s, 900 s, 1 h, 6 h, 12 h).
  \item[$\operatorname{mid}_m(t)$] the mid price of market $m$ at the last book update strictly before $t$.
  \item[$y_{m,t}$] the label: \textsc{up}, \textsc{const} or \textsc{down} according to whether $\operatorname{mid}_m(t + H)$ is above, equal to or below $\operatorname{mid}_m(t)$ (\cref{eq:label}).
  \item[$(m, t)$] a sample: market $m$ at block $t$, a block in which $m$ had a book event.
  \item[$\Delta^{-}_{L}$] the change of the mid over the past lookback $L$.
  \item[$\mathbf{x}_{m,t}$] the 61 hand-made features of sample $(m, t)$.
  \item[$\mathbf{z}_{m,t}$, $\hat{\mathbf{p}}_{m,t}$, $\hat{y}_{m,t}$] the three logits, the three probabilities and the predicted class of a model at $(m, t)$; $\boldsymbol{\delta}$ the calibration offsets added to the logits (\cref{eq:predictor}).
  \item[$\mathbf{h}_m$, $\mathbf{h}_w$] the per-block states of market $m$ and wallet $w$, 128 numbers each; $\mathbf{h}^{\min}_m$, $\mathbf{h}^{\mathrm{hr}}_m$ the minute and hour states; $\bar{\mathbf{h}}$ the global state (\cref{eq:state-update,eq:memory}).
  \item[$r$] the learned decay rate of the states; $e^{-r\Delta t}$ the decay over $\Delta t$ seconds.
  \item[$K$, $\mathbf{W}_{m,t}$] the number of book states in the window read by the encoder, and the window itself; $\phi$ the window encoder.
  \item[$G = (V, E)$] a graph with nodes $V$ and edges $E$; $E_t$ the fills of block $t$, $N_t(v)$ those incident to node $v$, $\mathbf{a}_e$ the seven attributes of fill $e$ (\cref{eq:mp-attention,eq:mp-update}).
  \item[$\mathrm{BA}$, $\mathrm{recall}_c$] balanced accuracy and the recall of class $c$ (\cref{sec:bg:eval}).
  \item[$\Delta$] a difference of balanced accuracies in percentage points, followed by its 95\% interval in brackets.
  \item[G1 \ldots G5] the nested groups of hand-made features (\cref{app:features}).
\end{description}
