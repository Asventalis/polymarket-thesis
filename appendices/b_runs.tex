\chapter{Run Labels and Configurations}
\label{app:runs}

Every stateful run is identified by a label that encodes its configuration, and every
number in the thesis points to one.  The grammar of the labels of the final study
(\path{run_hand_runs_v4.sh}) is
\begin{center}
\path{<H>_lb_hm_sg_lb5_<enc><K>_hand[_xp][_tree[_tro]][_ep<N>][_ema<d>]_ablation_pred_s<seed>}
\end{center}
with the components of \cref{tab:app:labels}.  Earlier runs use subsets of the same
components (\path{1h_lb_pred_s0} is the lookback model with the graph,
\path{1h_lb_ablation_pred_s0} its LOB-only counterpart, \path{1h_lb_hm_sg_lb5_ablation_pred_s0}
the best LOB-only model before the time-first encoder).  Validation predictions are written
to \path{val_predictions_<label>.parquet}, one row per sample with the time, the market,
the true and predicted class, the probabilities and the deduplication flag, so that any two
runs can be compared on exactly the same samples.

\section*{Training configuration of the stateful model}

State dimension 128; per-block, minute and hour states per market, one per-block state per
wallet; learned shared decay rate (converges to about $0.04\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$); window of
$K$ book states, 52 tensor features plus 3 within-window momentum columns plus the lookback
and hand columns; time-first encoder with a shared $\mathbb{R}^{K} \to \mathbb{R}^{32}$ time
map and one self-attention layer over the channels; predictor MLP of two layers of 64 units
with dropout; focal loss $\gamma = 2$ with class weights 2/1/2; AdamW, learning rate
$10^{-4}$; truncated backpropagation through five consecutive blocks; one or two passes over
6--12 March in time order; validation on 13 March.  Runs take 9 to 17 hours on one GPU of
the shared server depending on the horizon and the load.

\section*{Configuration of the stateless control}

LightGBM multiclass with class-balanced weights, learning rate 0.05, 63 leaves, at least 500
rows per leaf, feature and row subsampling 0.8, L2 regularisation 1, at most 600 rounds with
early stopping (patience 40) on 12 March, seed 0; fit rows: one tensor row in ten of 7--11
March whose market belongs to the model's universe and whose last event is at or after
$t + H$; validation samples: those of the stateful run at the same horizon.  The tabular
networks (MLP and TabM-mini, $k = 16$) use three layers of 512 units, a piecewise-linear
encoding with 16 quantile bins, dropout 0.15, batches of 8{,}192 rows, a one-cycle schedule
at peak rate $10^{-3}$ over 16 epochs with patience 4, class-balanced cross-entropy, seeds
0--2.
