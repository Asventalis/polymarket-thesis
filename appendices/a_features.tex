\chapter{The Sixty-One Hand-Made Features}
\label{app:features}

The features of the stateless control (\cref{sec:prep:features}) are computed for every
tensor row from the clean data strictly before the row's time, with DuckDB window functions
over the market's own history (\texttt{hand\_features.py}).  Sizes are in contract units,
times in seconds, prices in ticks of the market where noted; \texttt{log} is $\ln(1 + x)$.
The category is a one-hot indicator in the networks and a categorical column in the trees.

   & \texttt{range\_pos\_60m} & position of the mid in its one-hour range, centred at zero \\
